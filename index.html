01 / ABOUT THE LAB

Research with a
hardware mindset.

IC Design Lab is an academic research group focused on integrated circuit design and computer hardware systems at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology.

We investigate hardware architectures, security primitives, FPGA implementations, and emerging methods for efficient computing. Our work connects fundamental ideas with demonstrable designs and open research artifacts.
